The Story

Wild Hare Moon began with a love of working by hand and a deep connection to the old ways. Every piece Heather creates carries a bit of moonlight and a bit of magic — made slowly, with intention, the way it's always been done. Whether she's crafting something new or teaching someone else the craft, it all comes from the same quiet place: honoring the cycles, the seasons, and the wild things that guide us.
